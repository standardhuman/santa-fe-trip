## 🎨 Things to Do in Santa Fe

### Outdoor Adventures
- **⛷️ Ski Santa Fe** - 16 miles from downtown, 86 trails, 225" snowfall
- **❄️ Snowshoeing** - Forest trails perfect for winter
- **⛰️ Atalaya Mountain Trail** - 6-mile round trip, 1,600 ft elevation gain
- **🥾 Dale Ball Trails** - 25-mile network, great winter hiking

### Arts & Culture
- **🎭 Meow Wolf** - Immersive art experience, House of Eternal Return
- **🖼️ Georgia O'Keeffe Museum** - World's largest O'Keeffe collection
- **🎨 Canyon Road Galleries** - Over 100 galleries in half mile
- **🎄 Santa Fe Plaza** - Historic downtown with holiday lighting

### Relaxation & Holiday Experiences
- **🧖 Ten Thousand Waves** - Japanese-inspired spa with forest hot tubs
- **✨ Santa Fe Botanical Garden GLOW** - Holiday light show with lasers
