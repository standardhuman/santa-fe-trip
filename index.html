🎄 Santa Fe Holiday Trip

December 27-31, 2025 • Trip Planning Options

✨ Trip Overview

A 5-day holiday getaway to Santa Fe, New Mexico from December 27-31, 2025. Fly direct from San Francisco on United Airlines, arriving late Friday evening and returning Tuesday morning on New Year's Eve.

▸ Read more...

The trip centers around a special birthday celebration on December 29 for Allison's dad and brother, with three full days in Santa Fe for winter activities like skiing at Ski Santa Fe, exploring iconic cultural destinations like Meow Wolf and the Georgia O'Keeffe Museum, and enjoying the festive atmosphere of Canyon Road galleries and the Botanical Garden's holiday light show. Smart logistics include staying at Albuquerque airport hotels on arrival and departure nights to avoid early morning drives, with a rental car providing flexibility to explore the Santa Fe National Forest and surrounding areas. Trip costs range from $647-$704 per person for couples, split across budget, recommended, and premium tiers.

🎨 Things to Do in Santa Fe

⛷️ Outdoor Adventures

⛷️ Ski Santa Fe

Just 16 miles from downtown! Base elevation 10,350 ft with 86 trails for all levels. 225" average snowfall.

Visit Website →

❄️ Snowshoeing Adventures

Winter trails at higher elevations. Forest Service roads closed to vehicles make perfect snowshoeing routes.

Winter Sports →

⛰️ Atalaya Mountain Trail

Popular 6-mile round trip hike with incredible panoramic views of Santa Fe. Moderate difficulty, elevation gain 1,600 ft.

View Trail →

🥾 Dale Ball Trails

25-mile network of multi-use trails with stunning views. Great for winter hiking with various difficulty levels.

Trail Info →

🌲 Santa Fe National Forest

Year-round hiking, snowshoeing, and Nordic skiing. Canyon hikes perfect for winter when mountain trails are icy.

Forest Info →

🎨 Arts & Culture

🎭 Meow Wolf

Immersive art experience. House of Eternal Return - a must-see interactive art installation.

Get Tickets →

🖼️ Georgia O'Keeffe Museum

World's largest collection of O'Keeffe's work. Iconic Santa Fe cultural destination.

Plan Visit →

🎨 Canyon Road Galleries

Over 100 art galleries and boutiques in a half mile. Perfect for art lovers and holiday shopping.

Explore →

🏛️ Museum of International Folk Art

World's largest collection of folk art. Fascinating cultural exhibits from around the globe.

Visit →

🎄 Santa Fe Plaza

Historic downtown plaza with holiday lighting, shopping, restaurants, and festive atmosphere.

Explore →
